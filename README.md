### Music Store Analysis | SQL

#### Project Overview
This project performs a deep-dive analysis of a digital music store's database to uncover business insights regarding customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and artist popularity. The goal is to assist the store in making data-driven decisions for promotional events and customer loyalty programs.

#### Tools Used
Database: MySQL
Language: SQL
Key Techniques: Multi-table Joins, Aggregations, Subqueries, Ordering, and Grouping.

#### Business Questions Addressed
The analysis focuses on three main areas:

1. Employee & Customer Insights
Seniority: Identified the senior-most employee based on job title levels.
Customer Loyalty: Found the "Best Customer" based on total spending across all invoices.
Email Marketing: Generated a list of all Rock Music listeners (emails, names, and genres) to target for specific marketing campaigns.

2. Sales & Geographical Analysis
Invoice Volume: Identified which countries have the highest number of invoices to understand market penetration.
Revenue Leaders: Calculated the top 3 invoice totals.
Event Planning: Identified the city with the highest sum of invoice totals to recommend a location for a promotional Music Festival.

3. Track & Artist Performance
Genre Dominance: Ranked the top 10 artists who have written the most "Rock" music tracks.
Technical Metrics: Identified all songs longer than the average track length to analyze listener preferences for long-form vs. short-form content.
